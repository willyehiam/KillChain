# Research Safety and Fidelity

## Objective

Represent modern strategic systems with enough fidelity to create meaningful
gameplay while avoiding false precision, brittle data, and an unnecessary real
world targeting database.

## Public information standard

Research should prioritize:

1. Official government publications.
2. International organizations.
3. Primary economic and infrastructure datasets.
4. Credible research institutions.
5. Publicly acknowledged installations and formations.
6. Commercial geospatial datasets with suitable licenses.
7. Reputable reporting with clear dates and methods.

## Sensitive detail policy

Use individual locations when they are widely public, strategically important,
and necessary for gameplay.

Use regional or synthetic networks when exact local representation is:

1. Unknown.
2. Constantly changing.
3. Unverifiable.
4. Not important to the player decision.
5. Unnecessarily sensitive.

## Uncertainty

The database must be able to say:

1. Unknown.
2. Estimated.
3. Reported.
4. Disputed.
5. Publicly identified.
6. Scenario designed.

It must never silently convert a weak source into exact truth.

## Unit of measure discipline

Keep distinct:

1. Sorties and airframes.
2. Peak presence and unique platforms.
3. Formations and launchers.
4. Munitions and delivery systems.
5. Budget and available capacity.
6. Nominal inventory and operational readiness.

## Synthetic world detail

Synthetic infrastructure and dispersed military locations can provide strategic
depth without pretending to reproduce classified or unknowable reality.

Synthetic data should preserve:

1. Regional capacity.
2. Network dependence.
3. Redundancy.
4. Recovery.
5. Geographic bottlenecks.
6. Strategic consequences.

## Moral and political systems

The game may permit reckless or abusive strategies, but it should model:

1. Civilian harm.
2. Legitimacy.
3. Law and authority.
4. Alliance effects.
5. Attribution.
6. Escalation.
7. Domestic opposition.
8. Long term instability.

The game should neither sanitize consequences nor turn real suffering into
decorative flavor.
