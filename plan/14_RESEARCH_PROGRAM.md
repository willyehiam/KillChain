# Research Program

This document defines the full future research stage and the public source corpus
workstream that is authorized to run during brainstorming.

## Research objective

Construct a playable, sourced, uncertain, and maintainable model of the world at
the selected starting bookmark.

## Initial country cohort

The target is approximately the top 80 countries by GDP, with the final ranking
metric, reference year, and treatment of tied or disputed entries still open.

The corpus must not silently mix nominal GDP, purchasing power parity GDP, and
different source years. `TOP_80_COUNTRIES.md` in the research corpus owns the
cohort decision and version history.

## Playable world model

Research must support player decisions. It should not optimize for collecting the
largest possible number of coordinates.

Exact coordinates are useful only when they refer to a public, fixed object and
the source supports that precision. Mobile force reports must include an
observation time and must never be presented as a current live location after
their observation interval.

## Fidelity tiers

### Tier 01: Individually represented strategic assets

Publicly acknowledged major bases, fleets, ports, airfields, space facilities,
major generation, major refineries, major dams, major industrial facilities, and
other assets whose individual status changes national options.

### Tier 02: Regional networks

Electricity, communications, fuel, transportation, food, and local logistics
represented as geographic network capacity and dependencies.

### Tier 03: Synthetic sensitive detail

Plausible internal nodes, depots, wartime dispersal, command points, and local
network structure derived from public aggregates without pretending to know
exact real locations.

## Claim schema

Every claim should include:

1. Value.
2. Unit of measure.
3. Geography.
4. Date or interval.
5. Source.
6. Source type.
7. Confidence.
8. Whether it is direct, inferred, disputed, or designed.
9. Intended simulation use.
10. Review date.
