## Covert action and irregular warfare

Discussed possibilities include:

1. Supporting opposition or separatist groups.
2. Funding proxy organizations.
3. Covert sabotage.
4. Political influence.
5. Election influence.
6. Leadership targeting as a high consequence political action.
7. Special operations.
8. Covert infrastructure disruption.
9. Insurgency and counterinsurgency.

These mechanics require attribution, access, preparation, intelligence,
counterintelligence, legitimacy, escalation, blowback, and long term political
consequences.

## Deception and dual use

The founder specifically wants the game to represent ambiguous modern
capabilities such as:

1. Civilian ferries supporting military movement.
2. Containerized launch, drone, sensor, or aviation support concepts.
3. Merchant shipping used for surveillance or blockade pressure.
4. Civilian infrastructure with military utility.
5. Decoys.
6. Emission control.
7. Concealment in civilian traffic.

The game should model the strategic ambiguity and counterintelligence problem
without making every civilian object secretly military.

## Counterplay rule

Every powerful action must have:

1. Preconditions.
2. Cost.
3. Time.
4. Uncertainty.
5. Opportunity cost.
6. Detection risk.
7. Countermeasures.
8. Political consequences.
9. Recovery or adaptation.
