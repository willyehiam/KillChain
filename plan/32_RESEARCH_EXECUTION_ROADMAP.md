## Research completion definition

The research stage is complete when the corpus can support a coherent playable
opening world without pretending to know every real object.

Completion requires all of the following.

### Global baseline

1. All 80 ranked countries have a valid macro dossier at the bookmark.
2. All 11 strategic additions have the depth needed for their geopolitical role.
3. Every country has government, leadership, economy, population, territory,
   diplomacy, military capacity, infrastructure, and active commitment coverage
   at its assigned depth tier.
4. Every missing value is explicit rather than implied by absence.
5. Every dossier declares source date, review date, confidence, and maintenance
   status.

### Complete military accounting

1. Every represented country has a national military organization and inventory
   ledger.
2. Tier A and Tier B countries reconcile major platforms, formations, equipment
   pools, readiness states, public bases, and global commitments in greater
   depth.
3. Lower tier countries conserve national equipment categories and major
   formations without unnecessary individualization.
4. Major warships and other command relevant unique platforms are individually
   represented where public identity is supported.
5. Aircraft, vehicles, personnel, munitions, reserves, training, maintenance,
   storage, and construction use conserved pools where individual identity does
   not create a decision.
6. Theater deployments reference the national ledger rather than creating
   copies.
7. Every unresolved national inventory difference appears in a reconciliation
   exception report.

### Politics and continuity

1. Each playable country has its bookmark government and constitutional or
   practical succession structure.
2. Tier A and Tier B countries have approximately 20 plausible political actors
   or an explicit reason a smaller roster is sufficient.
3. Elections, coups, deaths, resignations, party transitions, and breakaway
   regions can reference plausible institutions and actors.
4. Domestic war authority, legitimacy, and alliance obligations are documented
   as research inputs rather than invented universal rules.

### World geography and infrastructure

1. Every country has a province or equivalent strategic administrative layer.
2. Major cities, ports, airfields, bases, industrial regions, energy systems,
   transport corridors, communications links, and strategic waterways are
   represented at an appropriate abstraction.
3. Globally consequential public facilities are individual records where their
   status changes options.
4. Local grid, communications, logistics, and dispersed military detail is
   regional or synthetic when exact public representation is unsupported or
   unnecessary.
5. Network capacities, dependencies, redundancy, and recovery matter more than
   raw coordinate count.

### Conflict world

1. All nine fully modeled theaters have integrated political, military,
   economic, infrastructure, humanitarian, and international context.
2. All nine secondary crises can alter diplomacy, force allocation, economics,
   legitimacy, and escalation.
3. All eight political event chains have plausible actors, triggers, branches,
   consequences, and uncertainty.
4. Events after the bookmark remain reference trajectories and never initialize
   opening truth.
5. Theater force observations reconcile with country ledgers and dated
   deployment states.

### Quality and maintenance

1. Every domain has a validator or a documented reason it requires manual
   review.
2. Stable identifiers are unique and cross references resolve.
3. Contradictions are preserved and assigned a disposition.
4. Evidence critical to gameplay receives independent review.
5. A coverage report shows each required lane and depth tier.
6. A freshness report identifies records due for review.
7. A complete research gate report passes without blocking findings.

## Priority model

Work is ordered by dependency and player value, not by country rank alone.

Priority score considers:

1. Importance to the opening world.
2. Importance to a fully modeled theater.
3. Military and economic reach.
4. Alliance and basing centrality.
5. Infrastructure dependency.
6. Probability that uncertainty changes gameplay.
7. Reuse across multiple systems.
8. Research cost and source availability.

highest priority unblocked research waves without requesting routine approval.
