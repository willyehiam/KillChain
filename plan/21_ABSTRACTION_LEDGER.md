# Abstraction Ledger

This ledger records provisional decisions about simulation granularity.

## Player identity

### Representation

Strategic will of a state.

### Reason

Preserves broad Civilization and HOI4 agency while allowing institutional
friction.

### Status

Strong provisional decision.

## Time

### Representation

Real time with pause and variable speed.

### Reason

Supports persistent operations, simultaneous events, and HOI style pacing.

### Status

Decision.

## Global economy

### Representation

Country and regional stocks, flows, markets, capacity, trade routes, and major
firms or sectors where strategically important.

### Excluded detail

Individual consumer purchases and routine company operations.

### Status

Hypothesis.

## Cities

### Representation

Individual strategic cities with population, production, infrastructure,
politics, and network dependencies.

### Status

Hypothesis.

## Electricity

### Representation

Major public generation and regional grid capacity, transmission dependencies,
storage, imports, and recovery.

### Excluded detail

Exact representation of every substation and local circuit.

### Status

Strong research and design direction.

## Ground forces

### Representation

Formation level by default. Subordinate units appear in active theaters or close
zoom when they create decisions.

### Status

Open. Division, brigade, battalion, and hybrid models require design study.

## Naval forces

### Representation

Individual major warships and submarines, grouped into formations at distant
zoom. Smaller craft may aggregate.

### Status

Strong hypothesis.

## Air forces

### Representation

Units and mission packages at theater scale. Scarce strategic aircraft can be
individual. Sorties and airframes remain distinct.

### Status

Strong hypothesis.

## Space

### Representation

Important satellites individually or as named constellations, with orbit,
coverage, revisit, tasking, and degradation.

### Status

Strong hypothesis.

## Cyber

### Representation

Organizations, capabilities, accesses, campaigns, dependencies, discovery, and
recovery.

### Excluded detail

Typing commands or simulating packet traffic.

### Status

Hypothesis.

## Information warfare

### Representation

Audiences, beliefs, narratives, channels, credibility, reach, attribution, and
counteraction.

### Status

Hypothesis.

## Religion and culture

### Representation

Institutions, identities, coalitions, legitimacy, mobilization, diplomacy, and
social cohesion.

### Excluded detail

A generic faith currency detached from contemporary society.

### Status

Open.

## Weather

### Representation

Initially abstracted or omitted unless it changes a strategically important
mission.

### Status

Founder preference, provisional.

## Civilian traffic

### Representation

Aggregated flows at strategic zoom. Individual or clustered tracks in active
areas where traffic affects identification, access, risk, or congestion.

### Status

Strong hypothesis.

## Military logistics

### Representation

Routes, hubs, stocks, transport capacity, fuel, munitions, maintenance, access,
and throughput.

### Excluded detail

Manual routing of every truck.

### Status

Strong decision.

## Intelligence

### Representation

Sources, collection, ingestion, processing, models, hypotheses, confidence,
freshness, custody, provenance, and dissemination.

### Status

Defining feature.

## Mission execution

### Representation

Desired effect, actual executing and supporting assets, route, timing,
authorization, logistics, uncertainty, and assessment.

### Status

Defining feature.

## Historical events

### Representation

Starting momentum, plans, institutions, beliefs, and pressures. Outcomes emerge
from systems.

### Excluded detail

Forced events solely because they occurred historically.

### Status

Decision.
