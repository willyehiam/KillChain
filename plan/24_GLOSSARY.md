# Glossary

## Abstraction ledger

The record of what KillWeb simulates individually, aggregates, simplifies, or
omits, including the reason and decision status.

## Assessment

The process of determining what happened after an action. Assessment is evidence
and confidence, not automatic ground truth.

## Belief state

What a player, country, institution, or model believes about the world based on
available information.

## Bookmark

A dated global starting state.

## Command zoom

Movement between national strategy, theater command, and mission command while
remaining inside one simulation.

## Confidence

A calibrated expression of uncertainty about a claim or hypothesis.

## Custody

Maintaining sufficient continuity and quality on a track to support a decision.

## Decision advantage

The ability to make a useful decision earlier or better because information,
analysis, authority, and execution are connected.

## Desired effect

The intended change in behavior, capability, access, condition, or perception
that motivates an operation.

## Event

An authoritative recorded state transition.

## Formation

A military grouping that may contain subordinate units or platforms.

## Historical momentum

Starting plans, pressures, beliefs, deployments, and institutions that make some
outcomes more likely without forcing them.

## Invariant

A rule that must remain true in every valid simulation state.

## Kill chain

One sequence connecting detection, understanding, decision, tasking, engagement,
and assessment for a target or objective.

## KillWeb

The larger network of national power, intelligence, command, logistics,
platforms, effects, institutions, and consequences through which many engagement
chains exist.

## Mission package

A coordinated set of executing and supporting assets, capabilities, routes,
timing, authorities, constraints, and assessment tasks intended to create an
effect.

## Model accreditation

A statement of where an inference model is approved, tested, reliable, or
limited.

## Provenance

The origin, date, method, and chain of custody behind information or a game
claim.

## Strategic archetype

A reusable classification that helps countries share behavior while retaining
distinct data and plans.

## Truth state

The authoritative state used by the simulation.

## Track

A maintained hypothesis about an observed entity, formation, event, or pattern.

## Vertical slice

A bounded future build that connects a complete player decision through
simulation, interface, AI, consequences, and tests.
