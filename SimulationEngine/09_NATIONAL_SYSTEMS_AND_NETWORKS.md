economics, politics, society, technology, infrastructure, diplomacy, and war.

## Cross system causality

Example:

1. Diplomatic agreement grants base access.
2. Base access changes theater reach.
3. Reach enables a mission.
4. Mission consumes scarce munitions.
5. Munition depletion increases industrial demand.
6. Production requires imported inputs.
7. Adversary sanctions disrupt imports.
8. Shortage changes military plans.
9. Public costs reduce political support.
10. Leadership changes strategy.

The engine succeeds when these connections emerge from shared state rather than
special event scripts.
