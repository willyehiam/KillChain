# Operations and Mission Packages

## Operation hierarchy

1. National objective.
2. Theater campaign.
3. Operational plan.
4. Mission.
5. Task.
6. Platform action.
7. Effect.
8. Assessment.

## Campaign

A campaign contains:

1. Objective.
2. Theater.
3. Command.
4. Phases.
5. Forces.
6. Logistics.
7. Intelligence priorities.
8. Authorities.
9. Escalation constraints.
10. Success and termination conditions.

## Mission package

A mission package must reserve actual capabilities.

### Executing capability

The platform, unit, organization, cyber access, influence channel, or economic
instrument that creates the direct effect.

### Supporting capability

Sensors, command, tankers, escorts, electronic warfare, cyber, communications,
logistics, rescue, assessment, diplomacy, or coalition access.

### Route and timing

The package includes origin, path, timing, access, range, refueling, exposure,
and return.

### Authority

The package includes legal, political, command, and rules of engagement
requirements.

### Desired effect

The package is evaluated against an intended condition rather than only platform
destruction.

## Package conflict

The engine must detect:

1. Same platform assigned twice.
2. Same munition reserved twice.
3. Tanker overload.
4. Airfield congestion.
5. Sensor task conflict.
6. Route conflict.
7. Command path failure.
8. Authority expiration.
9. Logistics shortfall.

## Execution phases

1. Prepare.
2. Position.
3. Collect.
4. Launch or initiate.
5. Transit.
6. Execute.
7. Egress or conclude.
8. Recover.
9. Assess.

Different domains may use different phases while retaining a common contract.

## Countermeasures

1. Concealment.
2. Dispersal.
3. Mobility.
4. Decoys.
5. Jamming.
6. Cyber defense.
7. Air and missile defense.
8. Interception.
9. Civilian proximity.
10. Legal or political pressure.
11. Counterinfluence.
12. Rerouting.

## Damage and effects

Separate:

1. Physical damage.
2. Functional damage.
3. Mission kill.
4. Temporary disruption.
5. Behavioral effect.
6. Political effect.
7. Economic effect.
8. Information effect.
9. Collateral effect.

## Assessment

Assessment creates observations and beliefs. It does not directly reveal the
truth state.
