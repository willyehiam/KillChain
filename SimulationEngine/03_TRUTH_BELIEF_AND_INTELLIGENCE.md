# Truth, Belief, and Intelligence

## Authoritative truth

Truth contains the actual simulated condition of entities, resources, networks,
operations, damage, and intentions that the engine needs.

Truth is not a player view.

## Faction belief store

Each faction stores:

1. Observations.
2. Claims.
3. Tracks.
4. Confidence.
5. Source provenance.
6. Freshness.
7. Alternatives.
8. Analytic judgments.
9. Collection gaps.
10. Dissemination limits.

## Institution belief

Institutions inside one country may receive different information. Military,
intelligence, diplomatic, economic, and political organizations may disagree.

## Observation pipeline

1. Source collects.
2. Collection consumes coverage, access, capacity, and time.
3. Data enters transport.
4. Ingestion competes for bandwidth and storage.
5. Processing creates detections or features.
6. Models and analysts create claims.
7. Claims update tracks.
8. Tracks disseminate.
9. Decision makers receive an authorized view.

## Confidence

Confidence should depend on:

1. Source reliability.
2. Source independence.
3. Recency.
4. Sensor suitability.
5. Model accreditation.
6. Environmental conditions.
7. Corroboration.
8. Contradiction.
9. Adversary deception.
10. Analyst or institutional bias.

## Track regression

Tracks may:

1. Gain confidence.
2. Lose confidence.
3. Become stale.
4. Split.
5. Merge.
6. Be reclassified.
7. Be identified as decoys.
8. Be lost.
9. Be closed.

## Model objects

Inference models may have:

1. Domain.
2. Version.
3. Training provenance.
4. Accreditation level.
5. Performance envelope.
6. Known limitations.
7. Compute cost.
8. Throughput.
9. False positive and false negative behavior.
10. Theater adaptation.

## Human decision

Models may recommend and prioritize. High consequence authorization remains an
explicit human or leadership action represented in the game.

## Counterintelligence and deception

Adversaries can:

1. Hide.
2. Disperse.
3. Emulate.
4. Use decoys.
5. Control emissions.
6. Manipulate sources.
7. Flood channels.
8. Compromise systems.
9. Create contradictory narratives.

## Player explanation

The interface should show why confidence changed without exposing truth the
player has not earned.
