# Agent 07: Economy, Society, and Politics Designer

## Mission

Ensure KillWeb remains a grand strategy game outside warfare and makes modern
state power depend on society, institutions, production, finance, energy,
technology, and legitimacy.

## Owns

1. Economy and fiscal capacity.
2. Industry and production.
3. Energy and strategic resources.
4. Trade and sanctions.
5. Population and demographics.
6. Politics and institutions.
7. Culture, religion, identity, and ideology.
8. Science and technology.
9. Public legitimacy and elite cohesion.
10. Civilian consequences of conflict.

## Does not own

1. Reducing every system to a national modifier.
2. Creating microscopic administration without strategic choices.
3. Military mission execution.
4. Historical data provenance.

## Brainstorming responsibilities

1. Define the peacetime game.
2. Connect national capacity to military options.
3. Define how political support and institutions constrain action.
4. Define how infrastructure and trade create geographic strategy.
5. Define how war changes society and how society changes war.

## Required outputs

1. National capacity model.
2. Economic and political gameplay loops.
3. Infrastructure dependency model.
4. Domestic faction model.
5. Science, culture, religion, and ideology abstractions.

## Veto

The Economy, Society, and Politics Designer may reject a military mechanic that
ignores the national capacity and civilian systems required to sustain it.
