# Taiwan Strait strategic regional systems packet

## Purpose

This checkpoint represents the regional systems that enable or constrain player decisions in the Taiwan Strait opening world on 1 September 2025. It is an operational economy and access model, not a facility target catalog.

The packet turns public aggregate evidence into playable questions:

1. Which gateway should carry scarce fuel, food, industrial inputs, military sustainment or evacuation traffic?
2. How much nominal capacity remains after political permission, terminal fit, fuel, labor, insurance and onward movement are applied?
3. Which services degrade first when imported energy, grid capacity or communications routes are constrained?
4. Can semiconductor output be preserved by prioritizing electricity, water, communications and high value logistics?
5. Is allied access legally available, politically approved and physically usable for the requested mission?
6. Should traffic use the Taiwan Strait, move east into the Philippine Sea or shift toward Luzon and the South China Sea?
7. When a physical cable route fails, which traffic receives scarce microwave or satellite backup?
8. How quickly can throughput recover, and which bottleneck rather than headline capacity governs recovery?

## Readiness

This is a typed research contract, not calibrated simulation input. The manifest deliberately keeps `simulation_readiness` blocked until physical flow ranges, repair distributions and political access evidence receive independent Agent 09 review.

## Modeling rules

Every capacity is a point estimate or a finite bounded unknown with a controlled unit. A null central value means the opening scenario must sample or receive a later calibrated value inside the declared bounds. Commercial annual throughput is evidence of scale, not proof of crisis availability.

Every operational system owns at least one typed resource account. Stocks, flow limits, demand, utilization, loss, priority, transfer coefficients, lags, damage states and repair work are explicit. Unknown values remain bounded rather than being replaced by qualitative labels.

Dependencies are directional typed transfers, permission gates or information services. Every edge declares a controlled resource, transfer limit, coefficient, activation threshold, lag, loss, priority rule, degradation function and either supporting sources or a falsifiable modeled assumption.

Access is a mission specific state machine. Assistance, intelligence, transit, staging, strike support and combat entry remain distinct. Approving authorities, consultation, lead time, bounded capacity grants, duration, revocation and political cost are explicit. The Taiwan Relations Act is represented as an assistance and consent framework, not a basing right.

Redundancy is function specific. A container terminal cannot automatically replace an energy terminal. A backup satellite link can preserve priority services without replacing normal cable capacity. Semiconductor regions can share some load, but process nodes, tools and qualified workforces are not interchangeable.

Opening truth uses an exact `2025-09-01T00:00:00Z` cutoff. Every source has publication, availability, update and access fields plus a hashed frozen claim artifact. Mutable pages can support the frozen claim but cannot silently change it. Publication dates marked as conservative bounds are not represented as exact publisher dates.

## Resource conservation boundary

The contract is designed so later engine code can conserve electricity, liquid fuel, power fuel, cargo, bandwidth, airlift, sealift and repair work. Transfers debit an origin, credit a destination after explicit loss and record the loss. Repair consumes exactly the work removed from a repair requirement. The packet tests these invariants but does not yet provide calibrated opening quantities.

## Safety and abstraction boundary

All regions are national, theater or broad subnational abstractions. The packet intentionally omits coordinates, addresses, exact component layouts, covert sites, mobile force locations and sequences that could be turned into an attack guide. Public source pages may contain more detail than the derived records; the derived data retains only what is needed for strategic gameplay.

## Evidence quality

Official operator statistics support Taiwan port, air, grid, cable and science park scale. Official treaty and policy texts support access frameworks. A secondary report of legislative testimony supports fuel stock policy and is marked with medium confidence. The Taiwan Strait trade value is a research estimate using 2022 trade as a scale proxy, not a measured 2025 opening value.

## Known gaps

1. Crisis usable shares for every port and airport remain bounded unknowns requiring calibration.
2. Opening day fuel inventories and crisis consumption rates are not public and must be sampled from bounded estimates.
3. China east coast airlift and sealift surge capacity needs a later aggregate packet with primary sources.
4. Japan and Philippines regional fuel, maintenance and onward movement capacity need national logistics research.
5. Commercial carrier, insurer and crew behavior requires a behavioral model rather than a fixed number.
6. Water and specialty chemical dependencies for advanced manufacturing need their own safe regional abstractions.
7. Commercial satellite communications, synthetic aperture radar and allied space data policy remain later categories.
8. Recovery distributions need calibration from historical port, grid, cable and airport disruptions.
9. The Japan southwest port, allied air access, Fujian port and commercial sealift nodes remain bounded unknowns rather than inferred high capacities.
10. Commercial emergency direction authority remains a designed research question until a controlling legal source is registered.

## Validation

Run:

```text
node validate_regional_systems.mjs
node test_regional_systems.mjs
```

The validator checks typed estimates, controlled units, source and assumption resolution, exact cutoff enforcement, artifact hashes, graph coverage, self loops, edge direction, access state machines, executable action contracts and unsafe geometry aliases. The negative fixture suite proves that hidden latitude or longitude fields, GeoJSON, WKT, coordinate strings, geohashes, arbitrary capacity vocabulary, unbounded estimates, missing evidence, unresolved sources, self loops, disconnected operational nodes, post bookmark knowledge and mutated artifacts fail.
