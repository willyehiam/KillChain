## Opening truth firewall

Justice Mission 2025 is a later reference trajectory. It is named only in the dedicated future reference firewall so automated checks can prove that it did not leak into sources, claims, posture, exercise lineage, contradictions, triggers, force counts, or readiness.

## Force accounting

Every force reference resolves to an accepted organization in the canonical USA, China, or Taiwan force ledger. A reference to an organization does not imply a platform count, combat readiness, current deployment, or geographic position. No unresolved exceptions are accepted in this checkpoint.

## Count semantics

Daily aircraft numbers are sorties or observed activity instances. Ship numbers are vessels observed in a reporting window. Neither is converted into unique platform inventory. Exercise participants demonstrate practiced mission categories, not complete wartime readiness or likely success.

## Safety boundary

The packet omits coordinates, mobile positions, routes, covert lists, vulnerable component details, target sequences, and execution guidance. Crisis triggers operate at strategic decision level only.
