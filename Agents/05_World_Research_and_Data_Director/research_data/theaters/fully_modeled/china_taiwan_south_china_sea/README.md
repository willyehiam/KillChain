# China, Taiwan, and South China Sea Theater

1. Theater ID: `theater_china_taiwan_scs`.
2. Status: `collecting`.
3. Intended depth: fully modeled.
4. Coverage: Taiwan Strait, East and South China Seas, first and second island
   chains, and global intervention routes.
5. First benchmark: `justice_mission_2025/`.

## Active benchmark

Justice Mission 2025 now has:

1. A source registry.
2. The five exact publicly announced live fire polygons.
3. Two complete Taiwan MND activity reporting windows.
4. Correct sortie, platform, peak presence, and munition measures.
5. Atomic claims.
6. Explicit contradiction sets.
7. A validation report.

The benchmark remains collecting because later warning areas, named hulls, exact
rocket system identity, and firing unit detail require stronger source recovery.

## Theater research focus

1. Complete the Justice Mission 2025 benchmark.
2. Build daily baseline PLA, Coast Guard, and Taiwan activity patterns.
3. Research public command structures and force inventories.
4. Build the political and legal path from exercise to quarantine, blockade,
   intervention, and war.
5. Research United States, Japan, Philippines, Korea, ASEAN, NATO, Russia, and
   other relevant actor decision paths.
6. Build ports, airfields, tankers, sealift, merchant traffic, satellites, cables,
   energy, semiconductor, and logistics dependencies.
7. Build theater routes and reinforcement times without pretending mobile forces
   have permanent coordinates.
8. Preserve the distinction between actor claims, detected activity, inferred
   identity, and scenario design.

## Required scenario branches

1. Exercise ends on schedule.
2. Exercise extends without a declared blockade.
3. Maritime quarantine.
4. Selective customs or Coast Guard enforcement.
5. Port blockade.
6. Air and maritime exclusion.
7. Limited live fire escalation.
8. External military intervention.
9. Negotiated deescalation.
10. Amphibious invasion.
11. Long regional war.
12. Global economic and political spillover.

These are research requirements, not predetermined historical outcomes.
