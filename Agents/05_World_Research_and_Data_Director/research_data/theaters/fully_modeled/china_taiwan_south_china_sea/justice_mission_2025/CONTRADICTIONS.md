# Justice Mission 2025 Contradiction Register

## Why this exists

Conflicting numbers often describe different reporting windows, scopes, or units.
The corpus preserves those differences before deciding whether a contradiction is
real.

## Research rule

A scenario may not resolve an open contradiction merely because one value is more
convenient for gameplay. It must either:

1. Keep the identity hidden.
2. Use the best supported aggregate.
3. Use a synthetic replacement clearly labeled as designed.
4. Resolve the issue with stronger evidence.
