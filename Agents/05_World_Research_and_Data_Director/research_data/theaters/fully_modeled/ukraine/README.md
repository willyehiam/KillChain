# Ukraine Theater

1. Theater ID: `theater_ukraine`
2. Status: `queued`
3. Intended depth: fully modeled
4. Coverage: Russia's war against Ukraine and connected European security system

## Initial research focus

1. Political objectives, coalition commitments, and war termination positions.
2. Command structures and force generation.
3. Front geometry as a dated time series rather than one timeless line.
4. Air, missile, drone, maritime, cyber, and information campaigns.
5. Mobilization, manpower, production, repair, and foreign military assistance.
6. Rail, road, river, port, energy, and communications dependencies.
7. Sanctions, trade adaptation, and wider NATO and Russian posture.
