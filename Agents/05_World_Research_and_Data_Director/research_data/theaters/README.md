# Theater Dossiers

## Directory groups

1. `fully_modeled/`
2. `secondary_crises/`

## Dossier contract

Every theater dossier should eventually contain:

1. Scope and coverage window.
2. Actors and political objectives.
3. Historical timeline.
4. Command and organization.
5. Force presence by explicit unit of measure.
6. Publicly reported movement and deployments.
7. Operational geography and boundaries.
8. Bases, access, routes, and logistics.
9. Civilian systems and humanitarian conditions.
10. Diplomacy, sanctions, and outside powers.
11. Information and political narratives.
12. Escalation and termination paths.
13. Starting bookmark state.
14. Alternative history branch seeds.
15. Known gaps and contradictions.

Use `DOSSIER_TEMPLATE.md` when starting a new dossier.
