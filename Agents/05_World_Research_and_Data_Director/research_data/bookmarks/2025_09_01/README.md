# Global Fracture, 1 September 2025

## Authority

This directory is the machine readable authority for the first KillWeb campaign
bookmark.

World time: `2025-09-01T00:00:00Z`

Bookmark id: `bookmark_global_fracture_2025_09_01`

Status: frozen

## Why this date

The opening must occur before the major escalation paths that motivated the
campaign.

1. Justice Mission 2025 begins on 29 December 2025.
2. The United States operation in Venezuela occurs on 3 January 2026.
3. Operation Epic Fury begins on 28 February 2026.

Those events are useful historical comparison trajectories. They are not
preloaded outcomes and players do not receive foreknowledge of them.

The 1 September opening also captures active wars and crises in Ukraine, Gaza
and the Levant, Sudan, Myanmar, the Taiwan Strait, the Gulf, and the Caribbean.
It gives the player enough time to alter diplomacy, domestic support, posture,
readiness, intelligence, logistics, alliances, industry, and deterrence before
the later historical escalations.

## Temporal firewall

A claim may initialize the world only when it describes state valid at or before
the bookmark.

A publication released after the bookmark may be used only when it
retrospectively documents prebookmark state. It does not grant factions future
knowledge.

Any event after the bookmark is reference data for validation, AI comparison,
and alternate history analysis. It may never be silently converted into a
scripted event.

## Contents

`bookmark.json` freezes time, evidence policy, opening conditions, and selected
historical trajectories.

`sources.ndjson` records the sources used by that bookmark. Source records
state what each source can support and where corroboration is still required.

This bookmark is a coherent research boundary, not a claim that the full world
dataset is complete.
