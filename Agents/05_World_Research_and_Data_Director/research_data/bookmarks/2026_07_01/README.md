# Global Fracture, 1 July 2026

## Status

Frozen historical bookmark.

The world clock begins at `2026-07-01T00:00:00Z`.

This date freezes the temporal frame for research. It does not claim that the
world dataset is complete, and it does not predetermine what happens after the
clock starts.

## Why this date

1. It satisfies the approved summer 2026 opening window.
2. It is a clean UTC month boundary for reconciling national and global ledgers.
3. The June United States and Iran memorandum creates a fragile interval in which
   diplomacy, verification, shipping, force protection, sanctions, and renewed
   conflict are all plausible.
4. Justice Mission 2025 is recent enough to shape Taiwan Strait posture, while
   later July activity remains outside the initial state.
5. Ukraine, Gaza and the Levant, Sudan, Myanmar, Venezuela, and other crises keep
   the world active even if the player does nothing.
6. The date creates several historical comparison paths without turning them
   into scripted events.

## Temporal firewall

The simulation must distinguish three clocks.

1. `world_time` is the authoritative campaign clock.
2. `claim_time` says when a factual claim was true or observed.
3. `publication_time` says when a source became available.

A source published after 1 July may be used only when it retrospectively
documents state at or before the bookmark. It must retain its later publication
date.

An event after 1 July may be stored as a historical trajectory reference for
testing and authored historical behavior. It may never be inserted into the
opening state, disclosed to a faction as foreknowledge, or forced to occur.

## Initial research question

For every country and theater:

> What did the authoritative world contain at 00:00 UTC on 1 July 2026, what did
> each faction plausibly believe at that moment, and which important facts remain
> uncertain?

## Acceptance gate

This bookmark is ready for scenario initialization only after:

1. Every baseline country has a reconciled government and leadership state.
2. Every baseline country has a dated force ledger or explicit unknown pools.
3. Active wars have territorial control and actor snapshots.
4. Alliances, access, sanctions, and declared war authority are dated.
5. Strategic trade, energy, communications, and transport networks reconcile.
6. Tier A countries pass all eight coverage lanes.
7. Post start knowledge leakage tests pass.
8. The global state can be deterministically replayed from the bookmark.
