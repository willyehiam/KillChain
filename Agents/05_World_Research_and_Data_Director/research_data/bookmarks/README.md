# Historical Bookmarks

Historical bookmarks freeze a world time and an evidence policy.

They do not contain complete simulation state. They coordinate every country,
force, economy, conflict, market, alliance, sanctions, infrastructure, and
faction dataset so those records can later initialize one coherent world.

## Active bookmark

`2025_09_01/` is the frozen first campaign bookmark.

It begins before Justice Mission 2025, the January 2026 Venezuela operation,
and the February 2026 Iran campaign. Those later events are historical
comparison trajectories, not guaranteed outcomes.

## Rules

1. Every scenario input declares the bookmark it targets.
2. Every factual record declares when it was observed or valid.
3. Later publications may correct the past but may not leak future events into
   faction knowledge.
4. Historical events after the bookmark are comparison trajectories, not
   mandatory scripts.
5. Alternative bookmarks use separate directories and never mutate the meaning
   of an existing frozen bookmark.
6. A bookmark can be complete enough to play while still containing explicit
   unknowns.
