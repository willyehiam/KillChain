# South Korea force ledger

This directory is the national force accounting packet for South Korea at the 1 September 2025 opening bookmark.

## Current tranche

The first tranche establishes the public command skeleton needed before any force can be represented:

1. Republic of Korea Armed Forces
2. Ministry of National Defense
3. Joint Chiefs of Staff
4. Army, Navy, Air Force, and Marine Corps
5. United States Republic of Korea Combined Military Committee
6. United States Republic of Korea Combined Forces Command

The statutory source separates service administration from operational direction. Dated official testimony separates United Nations Command, Combined Forces Command, and United States Forces Korea authorities and states that Combined Forces Command receives policy, direction, and missions through a binational consensus process.

## Hard refusal rule

This packet does not allow a player or AI to summon a South Korean strike package.

Organization identity does not prove force assignment. Alliance membership does not prove political authorization. Combined command identity does not prove that any platform is ready, available, supported, or released for a Taiwan contingency.

## Deliberately unresolved

1. Active and reserve order of battle
2. Equipment and munitions inventories
3. Readiness, maintenance, training, and availability
4. Force assignment to operational commands
5. Basing and deployment
6. Fuel, spares, lift, sensing, communications, and other support
7. Legal and political release for missions outside the Korean Peninsula
