# Taiwan Civilian Access Infrastructure

This directory is the country level index for civilian infrastructure research at the 1 September 2025 bookmark.

1. `ports/` contains seven international commercial port access nodes and 84 monthly observations.
2. `airports/` contains seventeen civilian aviation access nodes and 204 monthly observations.
3. `rail/` contains 244 current station directory reference points, 239 calendar year 2021 station passenger observations, and 13 calendar year 2021 line freight observations.
4. `manifest.json` rolls up counts and provenance without merging the three domains.

Every child packet remains `collecting`. Access points are not complete facilities, observed utilization is not engineering capacity, and no record establishes military use, military absence, target status, damage state, or authorization.

The rail station directory was retrieved after the bookmark and is a quarantined current reference layer. It cannot initialize the 1 September 2025 opening state. Its historical passenger and freight observations remain separate from the directory, and line freight totals are never allocated to individual stations.
