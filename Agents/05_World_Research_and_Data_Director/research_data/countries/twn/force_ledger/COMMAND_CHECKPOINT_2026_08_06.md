# Taiwan National Force Command Checkpoint

## Disposition

Collecting and nonexecutable. Independent review has not passed.

## What is now resolved

The dated 2023 Republic of China National Defense Report directly supports the continuous Joint Operations Command Center and the principal public command families below:

1. Army theater operations centers, numbered Army commands, defense commands, Aviation and Special Forces Command, Education Training and Doctrine Development Command, and Logistics Command.
2. Navy Fleet Command, Marine Corps Command, Education Training and Doctrine Development Command, and Maintenance and Repair Command.
3. Air Combat Command, Air Defense and Missile Command, Education Training and Doctrine Development Command, and Maintenance and Support Command.

The packet distinguishes navigation containment, service force generation, and operational mission release. Service headquarters organize, train, and equip their commands. The JOCC operational path controls the Army theater operations centers, Navy Fleet Command, and Air Combat Command. Display parent fields grant no authority.

## What is not resolved

No exact headquarters coordinates, current assignments, platform movements, formation readiness, maintenance allocation, mobilization delay, sortie capacity, or executable mission availability is asserted. Aggregate command nodes do not imply a complete set of individually identified formations.

The 2024 United States Department of Defense Taiwan force tables are retained for the next inventory checkpoint as dated estimates. They will not be promoted into exact 1 September 2025 possession or readiness.
