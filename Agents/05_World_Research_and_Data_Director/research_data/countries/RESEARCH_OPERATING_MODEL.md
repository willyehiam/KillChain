# Country Research Operating Model

## Purpose

This directory turns country research into parallel work that can be merged without inventing facts or breaking inventory conservation.

The model applies first to the United States, China, and Taiwan, then scales to every country in the frozen cohort and mandatory strategic additions.

## Unit of work

Each country has eight research lanes plus one bookmark integration package. Every lane owns questions, sources, atomic claims, contradictions, normalized records, and an acceptance report.

Researchers do not write directly into an authoritative simulation state. They produce evidence records that are reviewed, reconciled, and only then proposed for later simulation adoption.

## Parallel workflow

1. Claim a country and lane work package.
2. Register sources before extracting facts.
3. Extract one proposition per claim.
4. Preserve time, measure, geography, and uncertainty.
5. Resolve identity without merging ambiguous entities.
6. Record contradictions rather than choosing a convenient number.
7. Reconcile totals inside the lane.
8. Run the country validator.
9. Submit the lane for review.
10. Integrate accepted records into the 1 September 2025 bookmark.

## Authority boundaries

1. A source registry records what was consulted, not what is true.
2. A claim registry records evidence, not final world state.
3. A lane may remain at shell or collecting indefinitely when public evidence is insufficient.
4. Empty arrays mean no accepted records have been added. They never mean the real world contains zero entities.
5. Exact coordinates require public evidence at that precision.
6. Mobile asset observations expire after their evidence window.
7. Post cutoff evidence may clarify a pre cutoff condition only when the claim explicitly states why it does not leak future knowledge.
8. Crisis rosters point into the complete national force ledger. They never replace it.

## Merge gates

A lane can move to needs review only when:

1. Every required question has a coverage disposition.
2. Every factual record cites at least one registered source.
3. All counts state their measure and scope.
4. Known contradictions are linked.
5. Unknown values remain explicit.
6. The 1 September 2025 validity boundary is enforced.
7. Quantity records reconcile or carry a documented exception.
8. A reviewer can trace every proposed simulation field back to atomic claims.

## Country completion

A Tier A country remains collecting until all eight lanes reach needs review, the force ledger reconciles, the bookmark state passes the knowledge firewall, and open contradictions are assessed for gameplay impact.
