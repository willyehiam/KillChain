# Tier A Country Foundation

## Status

The United States, China, and Taiwan now have canonical dossier manifests and
national force ledger shells for the 1 September 2025 bookmark.

These files establish identity, scope, completeness accounting, and data
contracts. They deliberately do not invent leaders, force counts, readiness, or
locations to make the folders look complete.

## Shared acceptance sequence

1. Government, constitutional authority, practical authority, and declared war
   pathways.
2. Twenty plausible political actors with dated offices, institutional bases,
   succession eligibility, coalition relationships, and source records.
3. Province level strategic geography and metropolitan, island, port, base, and
   corridor overlays.
4. Military ministries, joint commands, services, components, formations, and
   command relationships.
5. Complete quantity conserving equipment ledgers across every accounting state.
6. Public fixed facilities, home stations, access agreements, and logistics.
7. Economy, industry, energy, transport, communications, technology, and trade
   networks.
8. Alliances, sanctions, active crises, deployments, and faction belief state at
   the bookmark.
9. Contradiction review, staleness review, and global reconciliation.
10. Representation proposal for later game design.

## United States questions

1. What legal and political pathways permit defense of Taiwan or renewed action
   in Iran?
2. Which forces are globally committed, available, in maintenance, training, or
   reserve?
3. What can move from other fleets and combatant commands, how long does it take,
   and what risk does departure create?
4. Which bases, tankers, lift, munitions, allies, ports, and replenishment nodes
   make each mission possible?
5. How do Congress, public support, allies, services, and institutions constrain
   escalation without turning play into administrative work?

## China questions

1. How do party, state, Central Military Commission, theater, service, and local
   authorities connect?
2. How are the PLA, reserves, militia, Coast Guard, maritime militia, civilian
   shipping, and dual use industry distinguished and coordinated?
3. Which forces participated in Justice Mission 2025, and where were the rest of
   the national forces?
4. What forces can sustain a quarantine, blockade, strike campaign, amphibious
   operation, or wider regional deterrence posture?
5. How do economic exposure, domestic legitimacy, information control, industrial
   mobilization, and external intervention change those options?

## Taiwan questions

1. How do constitutional authority, civilian leadership, armed forces command,
   local government, opposition, and emergency powers interact?
2. Which active, reserve, coast guard, civil defense, and infrastructure
   organizations can mobilize, and at what readiness?
3. Which ports, airfields, energy systems, communications, food stocks, industry,
   and transport corridors determine endurance?
4. How do blockade pressure, ambiguous shipping control, cyber effects,
   information operations, and limited strikes change political choices?
5. Which foreign commitments are real, conditional, delayed, or uncertain at the
   bookmark?

## Anti slop rule

A country does not advance because prose exists. It advances when all eight
coverage lanes have traceable records, uncertainty, contradictions, review dates,
and a reconciled relationship to the global world state.
