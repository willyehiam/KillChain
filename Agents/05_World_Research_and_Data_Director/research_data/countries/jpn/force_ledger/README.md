# Japan force ledger

This directory is the national force accounting packet for Japan at the 1 September 2025 opening bookmark.

## Current tranche

The first accepted source is the Japanese Ministry of Defense 2025 white paper reference volume. It contributes 54 dated claims:

* 7 ground equipment or formation categories
* 31 aircraft categories
* 6 commissioned ship categories
* 10 authorized or actual personnel categories

The observations are dated 31 March 2025. They are not silently promoted into 1 September 2025 opening inventory.

## Hard accounting rule

A published count is not the same thing as an available combat unit. Every claim must pass through:

1. Intervening flow reconciliation between the observation date and the opening bookmark
2. Mutually exclusive custody and component accounting
3. Readiness, maintenance, training, reserve, and storage allocation
4. Command and support dependency assignment
5. Basing and deployment evidence
6. Independent validation

Until those steps pass, the packet is nonexecutable. It cannot create a strike package, reveal a platform location, or satisfy a support dependency.

## Important source semantics

Aircraft counts are property ledger registrations. Ship counts are commissioned vessels. Ground counts use the precision printed by the source. Personnel totals and their component rows are nested views and must never be added together.

## Deliberately unresolved

* Changes between 31 March and 1 September 2025
* Individual hull and tail identities
* Readiness and mission capable rates
* Maintenance and training allocations
* Base assignments and deployments
* Munitions, fuel, spares, crews, and command network availability
* Coast Guard, cyber, space, special operations, reserve, and civilian support inventories
