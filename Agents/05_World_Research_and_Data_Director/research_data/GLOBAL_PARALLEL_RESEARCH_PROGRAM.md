# Global Parallel Research Program

KillWeb does not research one country to completion before beginning another. All registered countries enter collection together across all eight research lanes.

## Operating model

The registry currently contains 91 countries. The country contract contains eight lanes. The generated queue therefore contains 728 independent work units. Every work unit may begin collection immediately.

Dependencies govern promotion, not collection. A military organization claim may be collected before the political authority lane is complete, but it cannot become an executable capability until command, custody, readiness, support, and authority are reconciled.

## Ten worker pools

1. Politics owns political actors, institutions, succession, and authority.
2. Economy owns production, trade, finance, resources, and national constraints.
3. Forces owns public military organization and inventory claims.
4. Facilities owns public fixed facilities and basing relationships.
5. Industry owns strategic production and conversion capacity.
6. Networks owns energy, transport, communications, and logistics.
7. Geography owns provinces, terrain, rivers, and maritime access.
8. Crises owns alliances, sanctions, deployments, and event chains.
9. Provenance audits sources, temporal validity, contradictions, and precision.
10. Integration performs deterministic merges, acceptance checks, and corpus status generation.

These are stable domain teams. They are not country teams. Each domain team processes regional shards so the same methods and abstractions are applied across the entire world.

## Regional shards

The 91 country registry is partitioned into eleven nonoverlapping regional shards. Shards are operational routing devices rather than claims about political alignment. Every registered country appears exactly once.

## Promotion model

Collection is massively parallel. Promotion is conservative. Dependencies never prevent discovery work from beginning.

No country packet, force ledger, facility record, or crisis claim becomes simulation state merely because it exists in the research corpus.

## Deterministic integration

Atomic claims merge in a stable order by country, lane, and claim identifier. Repeated identifiers must be canonically identical. Conflicting evidence is preserved as a contradiction rather than overwritten. Missing evidence remains unknown and never becomes zero.

## Safety and fidelity boundary

The corpus exists to support a historical and strategic world model. It excludes nonpublic sensitive targeting detail and live mobile force tracking. Exact geometry requires both a public source and a clear gameplay need. Strategic abstraction is preferred whenever microscopic precision does not create a meaningful player decision.

## Country specific escalation

Country specific deep work is an exception path. It is triggered by contradictions, opening scenario importance, unusual institutions, or a need for bespoke mechanics. It does not determine when the other countries begin research.
