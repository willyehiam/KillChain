# Top 80 Countries by 2025 Nominal GDP

## Frozen decision

KillWeb uses the International Monetary Fund April 2026 World Economic
Outlook vintage, indicator `NGDPD`, for 2025 GDP at current prices in
billions of United States dollars.

The cohort is frozen to this vintage. Future source revisions require a new
version rather than silently changing the roster.

## Inclusion rule

1. Rank sovereign states and Taiwan by the published 2025 value.
2. Exclude nonsovereign economies from the 80 country count.
3. Preserve excluded economies as strategic subnational entities.
4. Do not make gameplay substitutions inside the GDP ranking.
5. Add essential theater actors outside the cohort through a separate
   mandatory strategic addition list.

Hong Kong ranks 40 in the unfiltered IMF economy list and Puerto Rico ranks
68. They are excluded from the country count and represented under China and
the United States. Taiwan remains a distinct playable country and ranks 22.

## Strategic additions

The GDP cohort is not the entire active world. Countries required for
approved theaters remain fully modeled even when they fall below the cutoff
or lack a published IMF value. North Korea is the clearest example.

These additions do not displace any ranked country.

## Statistical caution

The source combines actual national data and IMF staff estimates according
to each country's latest available period. The machine readable roster
preserves that period for every country. GDP rank controls cohort membership
only. It does not determine military power, state capacity, game difficulty,
or country content depth.
