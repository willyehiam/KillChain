# Political Event Chains

## Purpose

Model political developments that can reshape the campaign even when they are not
primarily military conflicts.

## Event chain contract

Each chain should contain:

1. Historical background.
2. Actors and institutions.
3. Public demands and material interests.
4. Trigger conditions.
5. Decision points.
6. State responses.
7. Domestic and foreign influence.
8. Economic and legitimacy effects.
9. Escalation, reform, repression, fragmentation, and settlement branches.
10. Starting bookmark status.
11. Sources and unresolved disputes.

## Simulation rule

Political events are not deterministic flavor text. They alter institutions,
coalitions, legitimacy, policy capacity, economic behavior, foreign alignment,
and sometimes control.
